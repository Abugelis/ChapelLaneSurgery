About Us

Welcome to Chapel Lane Surgery, your local GP practice dedicated to providing compassionate, community-focused healthcare for individuals and families throughout Mallow and its neighbouring parishes. Our team are committed to delivering medical care in a warm, respectful, and confidential environment.

Our practice is rooted in the heart of the community, and we strive to create a welcoming space where every patient feels heard and supported. We aim to provide professional guidance, and continuity of care for all our patients. Our services include:

  • Comprehensive General Practice services
  • Acute and long-term illness management
  • Women’s, men’s, and children’s health
  • Vaccinations & immunisations
  • Health screening & preventative care
  • Chronic disease management and reviews
  • Repeat prescriptions and referrals
