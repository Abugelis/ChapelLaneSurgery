Our Team

At Chapel Lane Surgery, we pride ourselves on being a small, friendly, and approachable general practice where patients feel listened to, supported, and genuinely cared for. As a small close-knit team, we work together to provide high-quality, personalised healthcare to every individual and family who walks through our doors.

Dr. Marian Donovan leads our practice with warmth, dedication, and a strong commitment to patient-centred care. She believes in building trusted relationships with patients and supporting their health through every stage of life.

Our skilled Practice Nurses, Gemma and Mona, and GP Assistant, Ciara, play a vital role in the day-to-day care of our patients, offering support with chronic disease management, health checks, immunisations, and general nursing services. Known for a reassuring and compassionate approach, our nurses and GP Assistant ensure patients feel comfortable and confident in their care.

Often the first point of contact, our friendly reception team of Della, Dino and Linda, help keep Chapel Lane Surgery running smoothly. They are here to assist with appointments, enquiries, and general support, always aiming to make each interaction as welcoming and straightforward as possible.

At Chapel Lane Surgery, we understand that visiting a GP practice can feel overwhelming for some patients. That’s why we focus on creating a warm, supportive environment where you can speak openly and receive the care you need. As a small team, we get to know our patients personally and work together to offer continuity, trust, and a truly patient-focused experience.​
